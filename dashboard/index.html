THE PROBLEM

Every zone runs on its own

Commercial HVAC is typically controlled by fixed schedules and independent PID loops per zone. Without shared context - what neighboring zones need, what the weather is about to do, how much headroom the grid has - the plant ends up fighting itself. That isolation commonly wastes up to 30% of total energy consumption.

THE SOLUTION

Local proposals, central arbitration

Each zone gets its own lightweight agent that watches local telemetry and proposes changes when conditions call for it. Proposals travel over the Model Context Protocol to a coordinator, which reconciles competing requests against a sitewide demand cap and forecast data before dispatching any command.
