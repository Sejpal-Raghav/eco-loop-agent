The Problem

Commercial Buildings are Wasting 30% of their Energy.

Today's HVAC systems are static and disconnected. They rely on fixed schedules and primitive thermostats, unable to dynamically negotiate energy usage across massive facilities. The result? High costs, excessive carbon emissions, and uncomfortable environments.

Eco-Loop solves this using a multi-agent cognitive architecture. Autonomous LLM agents coordinate to predict, plan, and precisely distribute energy across the building in real-time.
