Eco-Loop

A swarm of autonomous LLM agents thinking, negotiating, and optimizing your building's energy in real time.

The Problem

Commercial buildings account for a massive portion of global energy consumption. The fundamental issue is that modern HVAC systems are incredibly static. They rely on rigid schedules, simple if-then rules, and isolated thermostats that do not communicate with one another.

Because these systems cannot predict weather changes, track grid carbon intensity, or negotiate energy usage dynamically across different zones in real-time, they end up wasting about 30% of their energy. This leads to high costs and an unnecessary environmental impact.

The Solution

Eco-Loop solves this by replacing static rules with a dynamic, multi-agent artificial intelligence architecture. Instead of dumb thermostats, the building is controlled by a swarm of autonomous agents that act like a digital nervous system.

These agents perceive their environment, negotiate with one another, and make complex decisions to optimize comfort while strictly capping energy demand and reducing carbon emissions.

Architecture Details

Eco-Loop operates on a strict, 5-layer hierarchical workflow executed every simulation tick (30 minutes).

1. Perception Layer

Always Active

Reads raw 1-minute sensor data. The State Compressor condenses this into 30-minute snapshots, while the Anomaly Detector filters out broken or impossible sensor readings.

2. Memory Layer

Persistent

Injects the results of past decisions and feedback into the current prompts. Agents read this feedback and self-correct on the fly without needing retraining.

3. Planning Layer

Every 6 Hours

The Forecast Planner analyzes 24-hour weather and grid carbon curves to set a high-level strategy (e.g. pre-cooling) and enforces a strict peak energy cap (kW) for the facility.

4. Reasoning Layer

Every Tick

Five independent Zone Agents propose setpoints in parallel. A master Coordinator collects these proposals and negotiates compromises if total demand exceeds the Planner's cap.

5. Safety Layer

Hard Veto

The Comfort Auditor issues emergency overrides if PMV bounds are violated. The deterministic Guardrail Engine clamps sudden temperature spikes before commands hit the actuators.
