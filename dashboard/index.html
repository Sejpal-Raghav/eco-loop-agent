The Problem

Modern commercial HVAC systems are fundamentally reactive and isolated. Controlled by rigid PID loops and static schedules, independent zones compete for central plant resources without global context. This lack of cooperative negotiation and predictive awareness (weather, grid carbon intensity) results in massive thermodynamic inefficiencies and up to 30% wasted energy consumption.

The Solution

Eco-Loop replaces static control heuristics with a distributed, multi-agent LLM swarm acting as a cognitive nervous system. Using the Model Context Protocol (MCP), autonomous agents dynamically perceive localized zone states, negotiate setpoint proposals under load pressure, and execute synchronized strategies to optimize the facility's global thermodynamic state while enforcing strict peak-demand caps.

The Architecture

  • 1. Perception: Compresses raw telemetry and filters anomalies.
  • 2. Memory: Injects historical state-action feedback for zero-shot self-correction.
  • 3. Planning: Analyzes 24h forecast data to dictate global energy caps.
  • 4. Reasoning: Zone agents negotiate proposals via a master Coordinator.
  • 5. Safety: A deterministic Guardrail Engine clamps hallucinated setpoints before actuator execution.
